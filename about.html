About the Blog

The Story Behind the Stories

A space for slow thinking in a fast world. For questions without easy answers. For the human experience in all its messy, beautiful complexity.

My name is Elara Voss, and I have been writing about the human experience for as long as I can remember, which, as the essays on this site suggest, may not be as long or as reliable as I think.

Thoughts Through Time began in 2024 as a private journal. I was going through one of those periods of life that resists easy categorization, not quite a crisis, not quite a transformation, more like a slow dissolving of certainties I had carried for decades. I started writing to make sense of it. Somewhere along the way, the writing started making sense of me.

I published the first essay publicly in January 2025, half-expecting it to disappear into the vast indifference of the internet. Instead, something unexpected happened: people wrote back. Not many at first, but enough to suggest that the questions I was asking in the dark were the same questions others were asking in their own darkness.

What This Blog Is About

The short answer: what it means to be human, examined one essay at a time.

The longer answer: this blog explores the territory between the personal and the universal. Memory. Identity. Connection. Solitude. Grief. Joy. The way time changes us. The way we change time, or at least our experience of it. The ordinary moments that turn out to be extraordinary when you look at them closely enough.

Each essay is published on Wednesday. I chose Wednesday because it is the middle of the week, a hinge point between the momentum of Monday and the release of Friday. It felt like the right day for reflection.

Why "Thoughts Through Time"?

The name came to me during a conversation about how our thoughts are never really ours alone. They are shaped by everything that came before us, every book we read, every conversation we had, every silence we sat with. And they will shape what comes after us, in ways we cannot predict or control.

Every thought is a traveler through time, arriving from the past, living briefly in the present, and departing toward a future we will never see. This blog is an attempt to catch a few of those thoughts mid-journey and hold them up to the light.

Get in Touch

If something here resonated with you, or challenged you, or made you think of something you had not thought of before, I would love to hear from you. You can reach me at hello@thoughtsthroughtime.com, or leave a comment on any essay.

Thank you for reading. Thank you for being here. And thank you for carrying your own unsent letters, unfinished thoughts, and unresolved questions into this space. They are welcome here.

Elara Voss writes from a small apartment in a city that changes with the seasons. When she is not writing, she is reading, walking, or having long conversations with people who are not afraid of silence.
